About Bothell Power Washing

Local, licensed, and built around one thing: doing the job right.

Who We Are

Bothell Power Washing is a locally operated exterior cleaning company serving homeowners and businesses throughout Bothell, Kenmore, Woodinville, Kirkland, Sammamish, and the greater Eastside.

We started this company because we kept seeing the same problem: national franchise cleaners and Craigslist one-man-bands doing subpar work with consumer-grade equipment and no accountability. Homeowners were left with streaked driveways, damaged siding, and no one to call when something went wrong.

We run a different kind of operation. Professional equipment, surface-appropriate technique, proper insurance, and a genuine guarantee. We're from this area. We understand what Bothell's climate does to homes. And we stand behind every job we do.

Our Values

What we actually care about — not a marketing poster.

Honest Quoting

We give you a real price before we start, not a lowball estimate that climbs once we're on site. If the job is bigger than expected, we tell you before we proceed.

Right Technique for Each Surface

Composite decks, cedar siding, and concrete don't get the same treatment. We adjust pressure, nozzle, and cleaning agent for every surface type. No one-size approach.

Pacific Northwest Knowledge

Moss inhibitors. Soft-wash for roofs. Dryer duct fire hazards. We know this climate and what it demands from homeowners. That's local knowledge you don't get from a national chain.

Real Accountability

We carry full liability insurance and we back our work with a satisfaction guarantee. If something doesn't look right, we come back and fix it. No arguing, no hassle.
