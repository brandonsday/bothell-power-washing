Deck & Patio Cleaning in Bothell, WA

Wood and composite deck cleaning that removes embedded moss, mold, and grime — and preps your deck for staining or sealing.

Deck cleaning in Bothell is a yearly necessity — not an occasional luxury. Wood and composite decking in the Pacific Northwest absorbs moisture year-round, and the combination of shade, needle fall from Douglas fir and cedar trees, and Bothell's high annual rainfall creates ideal conditions for mold and moss to embed into every board. A deck that looks fine from inside your house may be dangerously slippery and actively rotting beneath the surface.

Wood Deck Cleaning in Bothell

We clean wood decks using calibrated pressure that opens the grain without raising fibers or causing fuzzing. The process removes surface moss, embedded mold, algae, and years of weathered gray from cedar, fir, and pressure-treated lumber. For decks being prepped for staining or sealing, we can also apply a wood brightener after washing to restore the natural wood tone and ensure the stain bonds properly.

Bothell wood decks that haven't been cleaned in 2–3 years often have moss root systems inside the grain and between deck boards. We treat those areas with a moss-killing solution prior to washing, so the cleaning removes dead moss rather than trying to blast live moss loose.

Composite Deck Cleaning

Composite decking requires lower pressure and specific cleaning agents. We reduce PSI to the manufacturer's recommended range and use composite-safe detergents that won't degrade the board surface or void warranties. Trex, TimberTech, Azek, and similar composite products all have different specifications — we know them.

Patio & Hardscape Cleaning

Concrete patios, stone patios, flagstone, and paver hardscapes adjacent to your deck get the same thorough treatment. We match cleaning method and pressure to the surface — high pressure on poured concrete, reduced and controlled on natural stone and pavers to avoid joint damage.

Deck Cleaning as Stain Prep

If you're planning to stain or seal your Bothell deck this season, pressure washing is the required first step. Stain applied over a dirty, gray, or algae-covered surface will peel within one season. A clean, brightened wood surface holds stain for 2–4 years. We time our deck cleaning to leave you a dry, properly opened surface ready for product application.
