Bothell Driveway Pressure Washing

Your driveway is the first thing visitors see. In Bothell, our wet climate means concrete driveways turn green with moss and algae within a season — especially on north-facing or shaded properties. Oil stains from cars, black tire marks, and years of dirt accumulate and are nearly impossible to remove without professional equipment.

We use commercial-grade pressure washing equipment to deep-clean driveways and walkways in Bothell, Kenmore, Woodinville, and surrounding areas. The difference between a DIY garden hose approach and professional pressure washing is significant — we remove what consumer equipment simply can't.

What We Remove

  • Green and black moss, algae, and lichen
  • Oil and grease stains
  • Tire marks and rubber transfer
  • Years of ground-in dirt and grime
  • Mildew and biological growth in expansion joints
  • Rust stains from sprinklers or metal objects

Surfaces We Clean

We clean all common driveway and walkway surfaces found in Bothell homes:

  • Concrete — most common in Bothell; responds best to pressure washing
  • Pavers — brick, concrete, and natural stone; requires careful pressure and technique to preserve joint sand
  • Stamped concrete — lower pressure to protect the surface coating and texture
  • Asphalt — cleaned with appropriate pressure to avoid surface damage
  • Gravel approaches & concrete borders — we clean the hard surfaces without disturbing loose material

Moss Prevention

After pressure washing, many Bothell homeowners add a moss inhibitor treatment to driveways and walkways. This surface treatment kills residual spores and slows regrowth — particularly valuable for shaded north-facing driveways that see limited sunlight. Ask about this add-on when you call for a quote.

Walkway & Sidewalk Cleaning

We clean front walkways, side-yard paths, back patio entries, and public-facing sidewalks. Mossy walkways are a slip hazard — especially in Bothell's wet months. We remove the growth and leave surfaces safe and clean. Many customers combine walkway cleaning with a full driveway clean for a single-visit price.
